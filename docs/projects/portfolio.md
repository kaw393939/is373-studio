# Your career-informed hosted portfolio

**Career research is the research stage of your portfolio.** Use actual employer descriptions to decide who the site serves, what it communicates and which evidence to feature. Build your own hosted website and integrate your individual RAG chat. These are two individual deliverables assessed together, alongside a separate group content project.

## Research before designing

Read three original employer postings spanning at least two role families. Include FDE as a reference and investigate alternatives such as AI engineering, AI product engineering, platform/MLOps, AI security, retrieval/data engineering or evaluation. Include an internship/junior opportunity when you can verify one. Senior reference roles can reveal skills to develop; they do not establish that you qualify now.

Keep employer/title, URL, access date, eligibility, location, required versus preferred qualifications, three responsibilities, one existing piece of evidence, one gap and a dated next action. Compare two directions and choose a provisional target. Keep the full reflection private; publish suitable professional goals and evidence.

[Independent career research handout](/handouts/career-research) · [Printable PDF](/handouts/IS373_Career_Research_to_Portfolio.pdf).

## Turn findings into design decisions

Write your target reader and what they should understand or do. Map three employer responsibilities to portfolio evidence. Compare two lightweight page or case-study layouts using the same content, then select one with a reason. You do not need to build two full sites.

Include an accurate introduction, individual RAG case study and working chat, group case study identifying your own contribution, code/demo links, suitable resume/GitHub/LinkedIn links and a professional contact route. Keep the research, architecture decisions and actual checks traceable. Label planned features, simulated data and learning projects honestly.

## Build and check

Reuse your existing FastAPI application, brand, server and CI where suitable. Public pages should work without requiring a recruiter to create an account. Keep any content-import or administration actions restricted on the server. Check readability, keyboard navigation, mobile layout, links and the chat's failure messages.

A real peer should try to identify your target role, find evidence of one skill and use your chat. Record their actual observation, make a useful change and retest. Keep one evolving individual record containing separate portfolio and RAG sections.
