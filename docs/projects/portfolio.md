# Deliverable 1 - Individual portfolio + career research

**Your product is your own hosted professional portfolio website. Your research is career research.** Investigate roles, choose a target reader and use employer evidence to decide the site's content, design and featured work.

Your individual RAG app is **Deliverable 2**. Your shared group content system is **Deliverable 3**. Feature both in portfolio case studies and identify your own contribution. The portfolio's success is whether a reader can understand your direction and find credible evidence of your capabilities.

## Research before designing

Read three original employer postings spanning at least two role families. Include FDE as a reference and investigate alternatives such as AI engineering, AI product engineering, platform/MLOps, AI security, retrieval/data engineering or evaluation. Include an internship/junior opportunity when you can verify one. Senior reference roles can reveal skills to develop; they do not establish that you qualify now.

Keep employer/title, URL, access date, eligibility, location, required versus preferred qualifications, three responsibilities, one existing piece of evidence, one gap and a dated next action. Compare two directions and choose a provisional target. Keep the full reflection private; publish suitable professional goals and evidence.

Use them to explore interests and find original employer sources, then make your own comparison and design decisions. Selected examples do not establish current availability or your eligibility.

## Turn findings into design decisions

Write your target reader and what they should understand or do. Map three employer responsibilities to portfolio evidence. Compare two lightweight page or case-study layouts using the same content, then select one with a reason. You do not need to build two full sites.

Include an accurate introduction, individual RAG case study and a link or embedded route to the working app, group case study identifying your own contribution, code/demo links, suitable resume/GitHub/LinkedIn links and a professional contact route. Keep the research, architecture decisions and actual checks traceable. Label planned features, simulated data and learning projects honestly.

## Build and check

Reuse your existing FastAPI application, brand, server and CI where suitable. Public pages should work without requiring a recruiter to create an account. Keep any content-import or administration actions restricted on the server. Check readability, keyboard navigation, mobile layout, links and access to the featured projects.

A real peer should try to identify your target role, find evidence of one skill and open a featured project. Record their actual observation, make a useful change and retest. Keep your evidence under **1. Individual portfolio + career research**. Keep technical app evidence under **2. Individual RAG app + technical research**, even when they share a record.
