**Try a task:** save a draft, switch the clearly labeled demo role and review it; or import the synthetic fixtures twice and explain which records were added, rejected or skipped.

The previews use **browser-only storage, simulated roles and synthetic data**. They demonstrate an interface and small transformations. They do not satisfy claims of secure authentication, a real backend, deployment or genuine teamwork. The practice assessments keep those gaps visible.
