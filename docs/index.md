## Start small with your RAG app

Research **both** vector and graph approaches; **implement vector, graph, or both** based on your question and evidence. A working hosted app needs real retrieval, a real model-generated answer, and usable source references. You do **not** have to build the instructor's Ordo platform, install LangChain, train a model, or pay for a new server.

For a manageable start, use about **five permitted short documents** and **five evaluation questions**, including one your documents cannot answer. Reuse your existing FastAPI, GitHub and Docker/HTTPS work where it fits.
