# Compare roles and choose a useful next step

**Optional professional preparation.** IS373 does not add a new graded portfolio or career reflection through this guide. Use this help to extend your existing portfolio and professional materials; follow Canvas if a separate activity is assigned.

FDE work connects a customer's problem with useful software and delivery. Related software, solutions and implementation roles can also be worth exploring. A course project is evidence to discuss, not proof that you meet every requirement.

## Make a small comparison

Open two **original employer postings** from the [internship leads](/career/internships) or [job examples](/career/jobs-and-pay). Keep brief private notes:

| Check | Record this |
| --- | --- |
| Source and timing | Employer/title, link, date checked, location, program dates and deadline. Use “not stated” or “not confirmed” honestly. |
| Fit | Actual eligibility and three tasks/skills; what interests you or does not fit. |
| Evidence and gap | One thing you can demonstrate, one missing skill and a bounded next action. |

Check availability and eligibility on the original page before applying. Employer dates are not course deadlines. Do not publish personal reflections or private circumstances just to fill a portfolio.

## Two fictional comparisons

These are short practice reflections, not completed research you can submit or claims that a student qualifies. They illustrate connecting a role to evidence and a next action.

### Example A — content work as evidence, not a job title

“I compared the [Palantir commercial FDE internship](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/4d29249a-d7e8-4c39-880d-3b35d7b2f6f6) with [Beacon Health’s FDE internship](https://www.beaconhealth.ai/team/forward-deployed-engineer-intern). I’m interested in the connection between business needs and software. My content app can show one workflow and permission checks; it does not prove production-scale delivery. This week I will make another student’s local setup reproducible and explain one rejected request. I still need to verify each employer’s eligibility and build broader experience.”

### Example B — compare building with helping people adopt

“I compared the [Google FDE internship](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/jobs/results/75665705386549958-forward-deployed-engineering-intern-bsms-summer-2027) with [MakiPeople’s AI implementation internship](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/maki/e315491d-2c1d-4656-a5a2-07ce158d5d40). I want to investigate both coding and implementation work rather than choosing by title. My group pipeline can show a source contract, my contribution and a checked result. I cannot call a local demo a deployed system. Next I will compare one real requirement with my evidence and ask a career advisor how to build the missing skill.”

## Turn it into a professional introduction

Use one accurate sentence: “I’m an NJIT student interested in [direction]. I built [small task]; my part was [work], and I checked [result]. I’m learning [gap].” Share a relevant link when invited.
