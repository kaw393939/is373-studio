# Career research starts your portfolio

Career research is part of your individual portfolio design process. Explore FDE, AI engineering, AI product engineering, platform/MLOps, AI security and related fields, then use verified employer evidence to choose a reader and project emphasis.

Read three original postings spanning at least two role families, compare two directions and map responsibilities to your actual evidence. Keep personal reflection private. Bring the research into the Nov 17 combined individual Plan review.
