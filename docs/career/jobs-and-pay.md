Monthly internship pay and annual base salaries cover different periods. Equity, benefits, tax, location, and experience also affect compensation. Do not multiply an internship rate into a guaranteed yearly salary.

## Three lines worth reading

**Palantir:** “Proficiency with one or more programming languages.” A working project and an explanation of its behavior are better evidence than a list of tools. [Source](https://jobs.lever.co/palantir/4d29249a-d7e8-4c39-880d-3b35d7b2f6f6).

**OpenAI:** “design and implement scalable solutions.” A professional system must handle use, changes, and failures beyond a classroom demo. [Source](https://openai.com/careers/forward-deployed-software-engineer-nyc-new-york-city/).

**Baseten:** “partner directly with customers.” Listen, test assumptions, explain decisions, and help people use what you build. [Source](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/baseten/84c1801c-1a65-49fb-aaaa-beeafd530e7e).
