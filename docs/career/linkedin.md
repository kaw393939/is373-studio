# Connect your interests to work you can show

**Optional professional preparation.** IS373 does not add a new graded portfolio or career reflection through this guide. Use this help to extend your existing portfolio and professional materials; follow Canvas if a separate activity is assigned.

Use your actual identity and experience. A free LinkedIn account is enough. “Interested in FDE work” describes a goal; it does not mean you have held that job.

## Short checklist

- [ ] My headline says I am a student and identifies an interest or next opportunity.
- [ ] Education has my actual degree and expected graduation date.
- [ ] My About section gives one specific project example and what I personally did.
- [ ] Project links lead to working evidence, with course work and unfinished work labeled.
- [ ] LinkedIn, GitHub, résumé and existing portfolio agree on names, dates and contributions.

## Two short examples

These fictional examples illustrate wording, not credentials to copy.

**Example A — headline and evidence**

> NJIT Student | Python & Content Applications | Exploring Software and Implementation Roles
>
> In IS373, I’m practicing how to specify, build and check a useful content workflow. My project record distinguishes local tests, CI and actual hosted evidence. I’m interested in software that helps people complete everyday work.

**Example B — short project description**

> IS373 group course project: a small content pipeline using permitted sample inputs. My work was parsing, validation and source trace; I reviewed a teammate’s output flow. The record identifies actual checks and labels unfinished integrations.

## Use it effectively

Follow a few employers from your career research and read what their roles require. After a useful NJIT or local event conversation, connect with the shared context and one relevant question. Ask before sending repeated follow-ups. A concise project link is more useful than an unsupported claim of expertise.

Check your profile URL as a visitor; LinkedIn may request sign-in. Use your own profile link, not an edit screen or a fictional draft. Add suitable links to your career-informed hosted portfolio. Reuse an existing portfolio where appropriate while meeting the current brief.
